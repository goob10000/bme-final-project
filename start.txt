The research question I plan to address is: "What species did SARS-CoV-2 most likey jump from into humans?" 
To do this, I will compare codon usages in highly expressed genes from a variety of organisms (starting with the ones deemed most likely).
I will need permutation testing to ensure that codon frequencies are truly significantly similar to COVID. I may also use PCA to look at relationships between certain codon usages and species/relation to COVID to get an idea of "distance." 
I will need data on gene expression from a bunch of different organisms (and the gene transcripts themselves) so I can look at the codon frequencies. 
Most of the data i've found so far is in FASTA format.
I will be working under the assumption that all genes in COVID are highly expressed.
I don't see getting data as a challenge so far. I will be getting most of my data from:
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/genome/
- https://www.kazusa.or.jp/codon/
- GEO
- Any place with bulk RNA seq data that I come across. This will help me get frequency and codon data.
